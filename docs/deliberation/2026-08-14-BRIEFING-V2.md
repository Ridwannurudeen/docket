# §3 — Claude's opening position (CHALLENGE THIS)

Explicitly separated from §1–§2 so it cannot contaminate the fact base. Everything below is
argument, not observation.

1. **Data Quality is the highest-leverage main-track gap, and it is a pure build.** The
   criterion's first word is "real-time"; we serve a 7-day-old snapshot with no refresh loop,
   which will be 33 days old at judging. Its stated blocker (the `finished_at` bug) is fixed,
   so it is unblocked. It also needs to run for *weeks* before the Sep 1–5 report re-run for a
   real-time claim to be credible.
2. **The 0.01 $U pricing is a live risk to TermiX's 30% "Value of the services" criterion.**
   Cheap makes the judge's hire frictionless (good) but a one-cent price may read as "demo, not
   a real working agent" on a criterion literally named *Value*. I do not think the fix is
   simply raising the price — a $70 price on a 30-second call invites a different objection.
   This needs a designed answer, and it is the single item I am least confident about.
3. **SOLVENT is the only place we are silent on something a sponsor named explicitly.** TermiX
   names win rate, window, risk. We publish anchor coverage and elapsed time. The agent is
   halted and honestly disclosed, which protects integrity but does not answer the criterion.
4. **Agent Diversity is met on paper but possibly not in substance.** BNB's category verbs are
   *manages*, *places*, *routes*, *protects*. Five of our six services are read-only and the
   sixth is preview-only. A judge reading the category table may find four advisors where the
   rubric describes four actors.
5. **PancakeSwap is winnable and currently under-claimed.** The brief explicitly permits
   analysis, and "without ever putting user funds at risk" is answered *structurally* by our
   no-key design rather than by promise — which is the strongest possible form of that answer.
   But zero volume has been routed, and a DEX sponsor asks "what did this bring us?"
6. **The BNB adoption question has never been answered in our narrative.** BNB is buying a
   growth funnel and asking "can we grow this?" Our submission has no answer to that framing.
7. **Housekeeping is a submission-blocking risk being carried too long.** Private repo, no
   README, no LICENSE, registration overdue. None of it is hard; all of it is fatal if missed.
